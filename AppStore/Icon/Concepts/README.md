## Prompt set

Shared request: creative iOS app icon review concept for CycleWrench, a bicycle
maintenance journal. The reference icon is bicycle-and-wrench identity inspiration
only; create a distinct new design, not a recolor or locked edit. Forest green may
anchor it, while cream, sage, terracotta, copper, warm orange and ochre/gold accents
are welcome. Multicolor is intentional. No saturated blue or neon.

Production constraints: single opaque 1024×1024 square, full-bleed background, no
baked outer rounded corners, no text, letters, app name, watermark or extra logos.
A pedal bicycle, never a motorcycle. Keep a strong central silhouette with visible
bicycle wheels and an unmistakable open-ended wrench. Polished gradients, texture
and material depth are desired, with Home Screen legibility.

### Enamel workshop badge

A premium glossy enamel workshop badge: deep forest-green glazed background with
a soft emerald-to-pine gradient; a raised warm-ivory pedal bicycle with a polished
brushed-brass open-ended wrench elegantly integrated diagonally into its frame.
Subtle beveled edges, delicate golden rim light, soft reflected warm highlights and
rich enamel depth. Bold simple small-size mark with close-up porcelain and metal
craftsmanship. Clean front-on emblem, not an angled product photo.

### Woven trail patch

A circular woven sage-green canvas patch on a full-bleed softly shaded oatmeal-and-
pine textile background. Large dark forest pedal-bicycle silhouette, muted terracotta
open-ended wrench integrated into its frame, restrained ochre stitching around the
internal patch edge. Cream ink accents, real cloth/paper grain, subtle imperfect
screen-print registration, warm natural light. Handmade outdoor-workshop personality.
The internal circular patch is a design element; the canvas remains square.

### Sculpted bicycle and tool

A playful but premium sculpted 3D miniature: a chunky warm-cream pedal bicycle,
two bold dark-forest circular tires, and a substantial burnished-copper open-ended
wrench resting diagonally through the frame. Soft ceramic and satin metal, rounded
dimensional forms, gold highlights, calm forest-to-muted-teal background and apricot
reflected light. Centered front three-quarter view with breathing room, feeling like
a beautifully made physical maintenance object. Avoid tiny mechanical clutter.
