## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production iOS app icon for CycleWrench, a simple bicycle maintenance journal.
Primary request: Design one exceptional, instantly recognizable bicycle-maintenance app icon. A clean white two-wheel bicycle silhouette whose diagonal frame tube subtly becomes an open-ended mechanic's wrench. The bicycle must unmistakably read as a pedal bicycle, not a motorbike; the wrench cue should feel built into the frame geometry. Contemporary native iOS craft, bold rounded strokes, extremely simple geometric mark, balanced negative space and strong small-size legibility.
Scene/backdrop: full-bleed opaque square with a rich vivid cobalt-blue background, a very restrained deeper blue gradient toward the bottom. The white symbol is centrally balanced with generous breathing room, occupying about 65% of the square.
Style/medium: precise polished flat graphic logo, restrained subtle depth at most; no excessive gloss, chrome, shadows, or photorealism.
Text: none.
Constraints: single square 1024-by-1024 image, opaque background covering every pixel, no transparency, no rounded outer corners (iOS applies its own mask), no border, no text, no letters, no watermark. No generic gears, no wings, no checkmarks, no extra symbols. Two clearly separated round bicycle wheels, both intact.
