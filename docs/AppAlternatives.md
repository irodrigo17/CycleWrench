# Free iOS bicycle-maintenance alternatives

Checked October 27, 2026 against official developer sites and US App Store
listings. This is a feature/pricing comparison, not hands-on testing. No apps
were installed and no rider data was accessed. Several candidates are small apps
with few public reviews, so their actual workflow and reliability remain untested.

## Best matches to try first

| App | What is free | Relevant capabilities | Main qualification |

## Useful free tier, with restrictions

**PedalCare** explicitly includes up to two bikes and core maintenance tracking
for free. Its listing reserves unlimited bikes, full service history, part usage
and cost insights, bike-health features, and Race Hub for Pro. It supports MTB and
e-bikes plus ride imports, but it is less compelling if full history must remain
free. The listing does not clearly define the exact free-history retention limit.

Source: [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pedalcare-bike-maintenance/id6755446215).

## Common recommendation that is not free on iOS

**ProBikeGarage** currently lists a **$3.99 upfront US iOS price**, with optional
Premium purchases. Its official site describes baseline tracking as free, but
that does not negate the iPhone download price. It is heavily Strava-oriented
and should not be presented as a free manual-first alternative.

Sources: [US App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/probikegarage-bicycle-tracker/id1422489202),
[official product/pricing page](https://www.probikegarage.com/).

## Recommendation

For this user's preferences, evaluate **velohaus** first, then **Velo Garage**.
Try **Loam** for MTB-specific servicing and its Bosch/EU implementation. None of
the checked official descriptions establishes CycleWrench's exact combination
of very short manual logging and averages learned from the rider's completed
replacement intervals. That is a documentation gap, not proof those apps lack it.
